Privacy and limits

What happens to your data?

Ablak has no account, no backend and no analytics. This page describes what the app actually does, including the parts that involve other companies.

In your browser

What stays on your device

  • The matching itself: the knowledge base and the matcher run entirely in the page.
  • Anything you type or tap.
  • The practice dialogues, the call simulator and the courier card. Courier details are not saved.
  • The history of recent phrases, kept only until you close the page.
Processed externally

What leaves your device

  • Spoken input. Your browser's own speech service transcribes it. In Chrome that service is run by Google. Ablak never receives or stores the audio.
  • Fonts. Manrope, Inter and IBM Plex Mono load from Google Fonts, so your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads.
  • Optional AI fallback. Off by default. If you turn it on with your own key, the text that was heard is sent to the provider you choose (Groq or Google).
  • Optional Whisper recognition. Off by default. If you turn it on in Settings with your own Groq key, a short recording of your speech is sent to Groq instead of your browser's speech service. Ablak does not store it.
  • Optional translation. If you add an AI key, the English or Russian text you say or type in "Say it in Hungarian" is sent to that provider to be translated, together with the place you selected (for example "bank"), so ambiguous words are read correctly. The result is labelled unverified.
  • The company hosting this page may keep ordinary server logs.
Stored

What is saved, if anything

  • Your language choice and whether "speak it aloud automatically" is on, in your browser's local storage.
  • If you enable the AI fallback or Whisper, your API key and that choice, also in local storage on your device only.
  • Nothing else. No cookies, no accounts, no analytics, no recordings.
If you do not want to use a speech service, type the phrase instead. It goes through the same matcher and nothing leaves your device.

What Ablak cannot do

  • It does not provide legal advice.
  • It does not provide medical diagnoses or treatment recommendations.
  • It cannot guarantee perfect speech recognition.
  • It cannot replace an official interpreter, especially for signing a form or consenting to treatment.
  • It may not understand every Hungarian expression. When it does not, it says so.
  • It is not an official service and is not connected to any Hungarian authority, bank or clinic.

Technical limitations

  • Browser. Voice input is supported in Chrome and Edge. Other browsers may not recognise Hungarian speech; use typing there.
  • Speech recognition. Accuracy drops in noisy rooms and with unclear speech. The optional Whisper engine can help, but it needs a free Groq key, is subject to Groq's free-tier limits, and sends your audio to Groq.
  • Translation into Hungarian. Prepared phrases are checked wording. Anything translated by a model is a machine translation: read the back-translation and, if unsure, show the Hungarian text to the clerk.
  • Internet. In Chrome, speech recognition needs a connection. The matcher itself does not, once the page has loaded.
  • External dependencies. Your browser's speech service, Google Fonts and, only if you enable it, an AI provider.
  • Hungarian wording. The phrases were generated by an AI assistant and checked for content by the author; a native Hungarian speaker has not yet reviewed the wording. Until that is done, treat Hungarian replies as suggestions.
  • Voices. Spoken Hungarian depends on the voices available on your device.
