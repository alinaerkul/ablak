About

Built from a real everyday problem.

I moved from Bishkek to Hungary and experienced how difficult everyday administrative tasks can become when you do not speak the local language.

I needed a dentist. At a state hospital I tried to book one with my TAJ card, but the receptionist spoke only Hungarian and a translation app did not help. I understood “third floor, one o’clock”, went up, waited an hour, never got the appointment, and left for work. In the end I waited for a trip to Kyrgyzstan and saw a dentist there.

Ablak started as an attempt to make these situations easier, not by translating words alone, but by helping people understand what is expected of them and what to do next.

Jany Jashoo

I also run Jany Jashoo, an information platform for Central Asian students arriving in Hungary, used by more than 100 students. Ablak could become a part of it.

This project

Ablak is my entry for the Hiya Voice AI Challenge (track: Real-Time Assistance). It is a working prototype, not a finished product. How it works and Privacy and limits say plainly what it can and cannot do.
